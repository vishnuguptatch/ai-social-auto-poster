PROJECT OVERVIEW:
This is an AI-powered social media automation system that automatically finds trending AI news, creates infographic-style images and captions, and posts them on multiple social media platforms without manual work.

MAIN GOAL:
To automate AI news posting workflow and maintain active social media presence with minimal human effort.

HOW THE AUTOMATION WORKS:

1. Every evening at 7:00 PM the automation gets triggered.

2. Manus AI starts the workflow.

3. Manus AI searches for the latest trending AI news from the internet.

4. The news data is sent to Make.com.

5. Make.com processes the data using multiple modules.

6. AI generates:
   - SEO-friendly caption
   - Infographic image
   - News summary

7. The generated image gets uploaded to Google Drive.

8. Caption is stored inside image description/metadata.

9. At around 7:10 PM:
   - LinkedIn post is published
   - Reddit post is published
   - Pinterest pin is published

10. After posting:
   - Temporary files are deleted from Google Drive
   - Workflow ends automatically
